# VenueX Core
  **Run your venue, not your paperwork.**
  VenueX Core is the all-in-one desktop app for **laser tag arenas and other gaming and leisure venues**. It brings check-in, game planning,
  customer management and marketing together in one touch-friendly app that's quick to use on a busy day.

  ## Who is it for?

  - **Laser tag arenas** and similar game venues with equipment-based sessions
  - **Venue operators** running one or several locations
  - **Front-desk staff** who need a fast, touch-optimized workflow at the counter

  ---

  ## What you can do

  ### 🎯 Game Planning
  - Assign players to vests with drag & drop, or drop a whole group at once
  - Plan up to three games side by side
  - Changes sync in real time across all devices in your venue

  ### 👥 Customers & Check-in
  - Customer profiles with visit history, groups and stored credit
  - Fast check-in with NFC cards (built in card printer and NFC Plugin)
  - Quick & Easy assign free drinks, coins or games

  ### 🎁 Promotions & Rewards
  - Reward your regulars automatically: birthday specials, first-visit bonuses, "X games per week" deals and more
  - Build your own promotions for your venue

  ### ✉️  Customer Mailings
  - Send newsletters and "We miss you!" campaigns to selected customer groups
  - Reusable templates with personalization

  ### 📊 Statistics
  - Sessions by weekday and time of day, new vs. returning customers and top customers
  - See where your customers come from with a map, top cities and top postal codes
  - much more...

  ### 🏆 Tournaments
  - Create tournaments in seconds: teams are drawn automatically and a full schedule is generated for you
  - Prefer to set teams yourself? Switch to manual mode

  ### 🦺 Equipment & Maintenance
  - Overview of your equipment: disable defective ones, flag them for observation and add notes
  - Maintenance checklists you can tailor to your own equipment

  ### 🕒 Staff
  - Digital timesheets for your team
  - Shift schedule as a calendar feed for any phone or calendar app

  ### ⚙️  Made for your venue
  - Manage several locations from one account
  - Light and dark mode, touch or hardware keyboard, multi-monitor support
  - Custom game modes and venue-specific icons
